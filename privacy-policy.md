# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 25, 2026_

fuegoCalc is a retirement planning calculator. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise process any personal information about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. fuegoCalc has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that observe you, and no servers that we operate. The app does not request your name, email, location, or any other identifier.

## What stays on your device

The retirement scenarios you create — your age, savings, spending plan, and any related inputs — are saved locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData persistence. They never leave your device unless you explicitly export them via the share sheet or, if you have iCloud sync enabled, sync them to your own private iCloud database. iCloud sync uses Apple's infrastructure and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## What we read from device APIs

- **User Defaults** (Apple's standard preferences storage): we use this for the active scenario ID, the selected tab, and the "has seen onboarding" flag. This data never leaves the device.
- **File timestamps** (temp directory): when you tap PDF or CSV export, the file is briefly written to the system temporary directory before being handed to the iOS share sheet. The system manages cleanup.

These are declared in the app's PrivacyInfo manifest as required by Apple.

## In-app purchases

If you purchase the Pro unlock, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information. Apple gives us only an anonymous receipt confirming entitlement.

## Children

fuegoCalc has no age-restricted content. We collect nothing, so there is nothing for parental consent to govern.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
